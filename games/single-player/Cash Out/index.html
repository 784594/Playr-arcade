HOW TO PLAY
🎯 OBJECTIVE
Go as deep as possible while building your value. Cash out to secure your earnings!
⚡ DEPTH
Each level deeper increases the stakes. Pressure builds as you descend—choose wisely.
💰 VALUE
Your potential earnings. Gambles can increase or decrease your value. Lose all HP = lose all value!
❤️ HP (Health)
You start with 100 HP. Bad gambles can damage you. Reach 0 HP = Game Over.
🔥 PRESSURE
Rises as you go deeper. High pressure worsens odds and increases damage taken.
🎲 GAMBLES
Each room offers risky choices. Some are safe, some deadly. Calculate the risk vs reward.
💸 CASH OUT
Available every 3 depths. Lock in your value and return to surface. Take it when it matters!
🛏️ REST SITES
Some rooms offer a chance to recover HP by spending value. Use them to survive longer runs.
📊 PROGRESSION
Unlock new gamble types and bonuses. Your best run multiplies rewards for future runs.
